The Emerson Table Tennis Club was founded as an initiative to build community charity table tennis tournaments across Massachusetts, with hopes for national expansion. Currently, the Foundation has two chapters: the Concord-Carlisle Chapter and the Lexington Chapter. 

Driven by our clear mission, bringing people together through table tennis while giving back to the community, we hope to provide a platform for those facing difficult circumstances to build the life they desire. 

Through our tournaments and outreach efforts, we have introduced over 200 people to the sport and raised over $3,000 in support of ovarian cancer research at Dana-Farber and Alzheimer’s research at Massachusetts General Hospital.

The foundation's premier chapter is the CCHS Concord-Carlisle Chapter; the CCHS Table Tennis Club was founded in the fall of 2023 at Concord-Carlisle High School. What began as a casual space for students to enjoy the sport has grown into one of the school’s largest and most active clubs.
